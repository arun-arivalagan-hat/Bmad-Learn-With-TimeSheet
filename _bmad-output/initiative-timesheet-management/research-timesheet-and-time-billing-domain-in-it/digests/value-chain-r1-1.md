## Claims

1. Flow: team members log time; PMs/delivery leads review against scope, billing rules, delivery; approver approves/rejects/returns (e.g. fix billing category or phase); approved time goes to finance for invoicing and to delivery/ops for utilization, project health, profitability. Source: https://birdviewpsa.com/blog/timesheet-approval-workflow-consulting-it-services/ | Birdview PSA | 2026-09-28 | med | vendor-blog (fetched). Owners: employee, PM, finance, ops/delivery.
2. The same page describes no lock/close step; month-end reconciliation appears only as a consequence of late approvals. Same source | med | vendor-blog (fetched).
3. Failures: delayed approvals bottleneck billing; retroactive end-of-week logging; unclear approval ownership between finance and delivery; spreadsheet/email workflows; disconnected time, approval and invoicing systems. Same source | med | vendor-blog (fetched).
4. Leakage = billable value earned in delivery but never collected, lost between work performed, hours approved, invoice issued, cash received. Realization = billed value / logged billable value. Causes: late/bulk entry, wrong codes, forgotten hours, slow approvals; scope absorbed without change order; write-downs without reason codes; quarterly billing; T&M unlogged hours, fixed-fee scope creep, retainer overage hidden in "included" hours. https://birdviewpsa.com/blog/reduce-revenue-leakage-professional-services/ | Birdview PSA | 2026-09-14 | med | vendor-blog (fetched). It gives no utilization formula.
5. Realization = hours billed / hours worked at standard rates. Vendor heuristics (no independent research cited): >3 days average lag between work and logging is a leak signal; 3 consecutive months over retainer entitlement suggests underpriced contract. https://synthelio.com/post/realization-rate-where-professional-services-fees-leak | Synthelio | 2026-09-07 | med (definition) / low (heuristics) | vendor-blog (fetched).
6. Utilization = billable hours / available hours. Search summaries cite SPI Research 2023 median realization 72-75%, top quartile >85%; utilization averages 66.4% (2025) or 68.9% from differing sources; leakage 5-12% of revenue. Sources: accountingdepartment.com, gigradar.io, sage.com blogs, birdviewpsa.com (URLs from search, not fetched) | various | 2025-2026 mix | low | search-summary. SPI 2023 figure is secondhand and sits at the edge of the 2-year freshness window.
7. NetSuite native timesheets classify billable / non-billable / productive non-billable at time-entry (line) level; billable entries flow to invoicing and billable utilization. Typical routing: PM approval, then finance review when billable hours exceed a threshold or unbillable time needs reclassification. https://www.brokenrubik.com/blog/netsuite-time-tracking-guide (and sikich/aaxonix) | partner blogs | n/d | low | search-summary, promotional partners.
8. Oracle NetSuite SuiteProjects Pro (OpenAir) docs: submitted timesheets "follow the normal approval process used in your company"; rejection of entries is an optional feature; timers create a timebill/charge; charges appear under Invoices > Charges; roles and permissions govern features. Billable-flag level and locking not covered in the page. https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite-openair/nsoa-online-help/chapter_N271613.html | Oracle | n/d | high (for quoted points) | vendor-doc (fetched).
9. Kantata: billable status can be set on tasks and projects; non-billable time tracked to give accurate margins; billable time, fixed fee and expense items are created, then invoiced in Kantata or exported to QuickBooks. https://knowledge.kantata.com/hc/en-us/articles/37158751310875-Glossary-of-Kantata-OX-Terms and /202806380 | Kantata | n/d | med | vendor-doc via search snippet (not fetched).
10. Billable at project level in Harvest: tasks on a Non-Billable project are non-billable; individual non-billable tasks may be added to billable projects. Projectworks has "non-billable timecodes within billable projects". https://support.getharvest.com/hc/en-us/articles/24510797704717 ; https://help.projectworks.com/knowledge/non-billable-timecodes-within-billable-projects | Harvest; Projectworks | n/d | med | vendor-doc via search snippet. Pattern: billability is a layered default (project, then task or time code, then entry override).
11. Locks and post-invoice adjustment: Harvest locks entries pulled onto an invoice; to edit you mark them uninvoiced (and withdraw approval first); the invoice itself does not update. Paymo auto-locks after N days. Project Insight has lock-down and grace periods, and unlock is admin plus Invoice Manager only. JoinedUp uses a "historic adjustment" tied to the original week instead of editing the locked record. MinuteDock allows no normal edit after a lockout date or invoicing. https://support.getharvest.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408204890381 ; https://help.paymoapp.com/en/articles/64829-how-to-lock-timesheets-after-submission ; https://support.projectinsight.com/hc/en-us/articles/360027083211 ; https://docs.joinedup.com/joinedup/msp-user/historical-adjustments.html | vendors | n/d | med | vendor-doc via search snippet.
12. Keka PSA: multi-level approval chains; optional client approval via portal (OTP), on a higher plan. Approved time becomes draft invoices automatically in integrated PSAs. https://help.keka.com/hc/en-us/articles/39946828609041 | Keka | n/d | low-med | vendor-doc via search snippet. Useful as an HRMS-adjacent comparator.
