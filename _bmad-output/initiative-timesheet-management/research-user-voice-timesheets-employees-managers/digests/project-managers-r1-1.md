   - Source URL: https://productideas.xero.com/forums/967118-payroll-expenses/suggestions/45667369-notification-reminder-to-employee-to-enter-times
   - Publisher: Xero (vendor-hosted user forum); date: Oct 2024 to Oct 2025 (within 18 months)

## Time figures stated
None found. No person in any retrieved source stated hours per week spent chasing or approving timesheets. The only quantities are vote/comment counts on the Xero idea (175 votes, 32 comments). The "XX hours" in a Xero comment is a placeholder, not a figure. One Xero comment says (via search summary, not confirmed on page) the commenter spent more time reminding people to submit timesheets and holidays than doing the pay run; no number given.

## Patterns
- Manual identification of who has not submitted, then individual chasing, is the named pain (Xero, 1 community). It is the payroll-processor role more than the project manager role.
- The wished-for job: automatic reminders to non-submitters only, shortfall-vs-expected-hours trigger, one escalation.
- Approval-side wish (Workfront, 1 community): hard limits so over-planned hours are caught at entry rather than at approval.
- Prevalence: nothing reaches two independent communities for the same claim with real user voice. Xero + Workfront overlap only loosely (chasing vs. over-hours). All claims are single-community observations.
