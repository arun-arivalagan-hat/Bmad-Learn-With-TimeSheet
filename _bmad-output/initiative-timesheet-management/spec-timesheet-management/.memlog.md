- (decision) Objective: capture actual work effort and link it to billing/utilization
- (capability) CAP-1 Employee selects the timesheet week being reported
- (capability) CAP-2 Employee logs effort on up to 5 projects: project, tasks/activities text, hours, reviewer; Project 1 is the primary project (most time)
- (capability) CAP-3 Employee records PTO hours (8 hrs per day)
- (capability) CAP-4 Employee records key accomplishments and key learnings with level (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced)
- (capability) CAP-5 Employee submits the sheet; name and email captured automatically and visible to the owner; all fields required
- (capability) CAP-6 (proposed, unconfirmed) Each project's reviewer approves or rejects hours submitted against that project
- (capability) CAP-7 (proposed, unconfirmed) Approved hours roll up by project, employee and week into billable vs non-billable and utilization
- (constraint) Per-project block has exactly four fields: project, tasks, hours, reviewer; max 5 blocks; Project 1 is primary
- (constraint) PTO is entered in hours at 8 hrs per day
- (constraint) Identity (name, email) comes from the signed-in user, never typed in the form
- (assumption) Only Project 1 is required; Projects 2-5 optional
- (assumption) Hours validation: 0.5 steps, PTO multiples of 8, project hours + PTO <= 40 per week
- (assumption) CAP-6 and CAP-7 are in scope though absent from the form

- (question) Can a submitted sheet be edited or resubmitted for the same week; is one sheet per employee per week enforced?
