## Claims

### Q1. Status of the four Labour Codes

1. All four Codes (Wages 2019, IR 2020, Social Security 2020, OSH 2020) were brought into force on 21 Nov 2025 by four notifications: S.O. 5322(E) Wages, 5321(E) OSH, 5320(E) IR, 5319(E) SS. Reportedly repealed 29 central laws. Code on Wages notification covers sections 1-41 and 43-66 (one source), i.e. possibly not every section.
   - Sources: https://ksandk.com/md/labour-employment/all-four-labour-codes-enforced-from-21-november-2025/ (K&S Partners, date not shown); https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2025/government-of-india-notifies-the-labour-codes-ushers-a-new-era-of-compliances (DLA Piper); https://blog.ipleaders.in/labour-codes-2026-implementation-complete-guide-to-what-is-in-force-what-changed-and-what-to-do-now/ (iPleaders, "last verified 8 May 2026")
   - Confidence: med (law-firm secondary, via search summary; not gazette). Class: regulatory status. sources_count: 3.

2. Final Central Rules under all four Codes were notified 8 May 2026 (Wages (Central) Rules 2026 = G.S.R. 343(E); OSH Central Rules 2026 = G.S.R. 345(E) per one search summary). Drafts were published 30 Dec 2025. Corrigendum G.S.R. 629(E) of 15 Jul 2026 corrected the Wages rules short title only.
   - Sources: https://prsindia.org/billtrack/code-on-wages-central-rules-2026 (PRS, not fetched, search listing only); https://www.bdo.in/en-gb/insights/alerts-updates/alert-final-central-rules-notified-under-all-four-labour-codes (BDO, 12 May 2026, fetched); KPMG flash alert https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/2026/flash-alert-2026-127.html (search listing only)
   - Confidence: med. Class: regulatory status. sources_count: 2 fetched/listed (BDO fetched confirms "final Central Rules notified under all four" but gave no dates; dates come from search summaries). The G.S.R. 345(E) number is single-source (search summary).

3. Many state rules were still pending as of May 2026 (BDO 12 May 2026; iPleaders 8 May 2026). Most private IT employers fall under state "appropriate government", so state rules/forms govern them. I did not find a current (Oct 2026) per-state status list.
   - Sources: BDO URL above; iPleaders URL above. Confidence: med (dated May 2026, about 5 months stale). Class: regulatory status. sources_count: 2.

4. Floor wage limits by skill category under the Code on Wages not yet notified (as of BDO 12 May 2026).
   - Source: BDO; single-source. Confidence: low-med.

### Q2. Record requirements

5. Wages (Central) Rules 2026, Rule 51: every employer keeps three registers, Form I (Employee Register), Form IV (Register of Wages, Overtime, Advances, Fines, Deductions for Damage/Loss), Form IX (Attendance Register-cum-Muster Roll), in electronic or physical form in the appended formats. Registers to be preserved five years after the date of last entry (Rule 51(4) per one summary). Wage slip Form V per Rule 52, electronic or physical, on/before wage payment. "Electronically" defined broadly in rule 2(1)(j) (email, portal, mobile app, website).
   - Sources: https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/wages-central-rules-2026-code-on-wages-chapter-wise-guide/ (Taxmann, 2026, fetched); https://taxclue.in/blog/rule-51-52-code-on-wages-central-rules-2026-registers-and-wage-slip/ (TaxClue, search summary only). Also PRS PDF https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/bills_parliament/2026/Wages_Rules_2026.pdf (could not parse; this is the primary text and the best place to verify).
   - Confidence: med (two secondary sources agree; primary text unread). Class: record retention constraint. sources_count: 2.
   - Caveat: applies to central-sphere establishments; where the State is the appropriate government, state rules apply.

6. Older regime: repealed Payment of Wages / Minimum Wages Central Rules had a 3-year retention (search summary); a separate unsourced 5-year figure circulates. Superseded for central sphere by item 5 but may persist in state rules. Section 50 of the Code on Wages requires records as prescribed and states no period itself.
   - Sources: search summary citing TaxClue section-50 page https://taxclue.in/blog/section-50-code-on-wages-2019-records-returns-and-notices/ ; Patron Accounting blog Apr 2026 suggests 7 years as practice (not law). Confidence: low-med. sources_count: 2 (weak).

7. Overtime under Code on Wages / Wages Rules: at least twice the normal rate of wages; hourly rate = daily rate / 8; monthly to daily = divide by 26. An overtime register is expected to capture date, normal hours, overtime hours with prescribed rounding, rate, amount (blog-level detail; the rounding rule itself was not found).
   - Sources: BDO (OT at 2x under Code on Wages and OSHWC Rules); search summary from Wages Rules commentary. Confidence: med for 2x; low for register column details and rounding. sources_count: 2 for 2x, 1 for the rest.

8. OSH Code section 25 (as paraphrased): no worker required or allowed to work more than 8 hours a day / 48 hours a week, subject to appropriate government limits; spread-over figures conflict across sources (10.5 vs 12 hours). Overtime requires worker consent; quarterly OT cap conflicting (144 vs 125 hours; some states stricter).
   - Sources: https://righttoinformation.wiki/working-hours-overtime-spread-over-osh-code-india ; https://ezhrm.in/overtime-rules-india-2026-labour-codes-changed/ (blogs, search summary). Confidence: low (sources disagree; official text unread). sources_count: 2, conflicting.

9. Service record per worker may be kept electronically or manually, updated periodically; working hours, holidays, pay days, wage rates to be published on employee portal/website in Hindi, English, local language (BDO, 12 May 2026; refers to industrial establishments). Single-source. Confidence: low-med.

### Q3. State Shops and Establishments Acts

10. Telangana: notification dated 7 Jun 2024 exempts IT/ITeS establishments from Telangana S&E Act 1988 secs 15 (opening/closing hours), 16 (daily/weekly hours), 23 (women), 31 (other holidays), through 30 May 2028. Conditions: weekly cap 48 hours, overtime payable beyond that, weekly off, integrated registers/returns, ID cards, compensatory holiday for holiday work, safeguards for night shifts of women; non-compliance may revoke exemption.
    - Sources: https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/labour-law-exemptions-for-indian-technology-and-outsourcing-employers-in-bangalore-hyderabad/ (AZB, 18 Jun 2024, fetched); DLA Piper Karnataka page and search summary mention the same Telangana 2024 notification. Confidence: med. Class: hours/overtime. sources_count: 2 (second via search summary). Note: current validity in Oct 2026 not checked.

11. Karnataka: AZB reports a 10 Jun 2024 notification exempting IT/ITeS from the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act 1946 through 10 Jun 2029 (conditions: report disciplinary actions to labour authorities; POSH and grievance committees). The AZB piece did not address hours or overtime. Karnataka S&CE Act baseline per compliance sources: 9 hours/day, 48/week, overtime at 2x; total incl. overtime 10 hours/day, 50 overtime hours/quarter. A 2025 proposal to move to 10 h/day, 12 h total, 144 OT hours/quarter was opposed by the IT union; outcome unconfirmed. A Karnataka S&CE (Amendment) Act 2026 reportedly got assent 3 Sep 2026 (service certificates within 7 days; no retention of original documents); its working-hour content not verified.
    - Sources: AZB (above); https://www.keka.com/compliance/acts/karnataka-shops-and-establishments-act ; https://knowledge.dlapiper.com/dlapiperknowledge/globalemploymentlatestdevelopments/2025/changes-proposed-to-the-shops-and-commercial-establishments-act-in-karnataka ; https://www.india-briefing.com/news/karnataka-shops-commercial-establishments-amendment-act-2026-47072.html/ (not fetched). Confidence: low-med. sources_count: 2-3, weak. The Karnataka IT hours exemption specifically was not found.

12. Maharashtra: S&E Act 2017 baseline 9 h/day, 48 h/week, overtime max 125 hours per 3 months, paid at 2x. A 2025 ordinance (effective 1 Oct 2025) raised daily limit to 10 h (incl. rest intervals), overtime cap to 144 h/quarter, overtime not compulsory, coverage threshold 10 to 20 workers. Bill 89 of 2025 introduced to replace it; passage not confirmed.
    - Sources: https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/bills_states/maharashtra/2025/Ord8of2025MH.pdf and Bill89of2025MH.pdf (PRS, listed, not fetched); https://www.lawrbit.com/article/maharashtra-shops-establishments-act-2025/ ; https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/19710/1/shops_and_establishments.pdf (listed). Confidence: med for baseline and ordinance (search summary). sources_count: 2-3. IT/ITeS-specific exemption: no authoritative source found.

13. Tamil Nadu: baseline 8 h/day, 48 h/week, overtime 2x (law-firm comparison table, https://employmentlaw.lkslaw.com/working-hours). State notification No. II(2)LWSD/441(a)2025 of 8 May 2025 allows 24x7 operation of all shops/establishments for 3 more years from 5 Jun 2025, with caps of 8/48 normal and overtime up to 10.5 h/day or 57 h/week (one source), women night work by consent with transport. No TN IT/ITeS-specific hours exemption found.
    - Sources: https://www.mbgcorp.com/indiatest/insights/shops-and-establishments-in-tamil-nadu-to-remain-open-for-24-hours-round-the-year/ ; https://www.indialaw.in/blog/labour/tn-allows-24x7-shop-operations-for-3-more-years// ; lkslaw table. Confidence: low-med (the 10.5/57 figures are single-source). sources_count: 2-3.

### Q4. Are salaried IT employees covered; daily or weekly recording

14. Several state S&E Acts exclude managerial/supervisory persons from their provisions (Karnataka and Maharashtra per one commentary; Delhi narrower, hours only; UP capped at 10 percent of staff). OSH-code commentary says the 48-hour cap applies to all establishments including IT/ITeS, and for monthly-paid staff the overtime test is the weekly 48-hour limit (blogs). Net reading: non-managerial salaried IT staff are generally covered; managerial/supervisory may be excluded in some states.
    - Sources: https://www.mondaq.com/india/employee-benefits-compensation/1445388/law-on-overtime-wages-under-the-se-acts-a-toothless-provision (Mondaq, 2024) ; https://humancapitalonline.com/Workplace/details/308/Policies For Leave And Working Hours ; ezhrm blog above. Confidence: low-med. sources_count: 2-3, none primary.

15. Form IX is an attendance register-cum-muster roll and Form IV records overtime hours; daily vs weekly recording requirement: the Rule 51 forms exist but I did not read the Form IX columns, so whether it mandates daily in/out times is unconfirmed. Overtime payment needs the hours worked beyond limits, which implies at least daily hours for those who may exceed limits (inference, not sourced).

### Q5. Correcting or locking approved time records

16. Nothing India-specific found. Only a signature note: Forms IV and IX need signature only if kept physically (TaxClue search summary; single-source, low).
