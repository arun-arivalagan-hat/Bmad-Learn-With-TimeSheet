# Rippling teardown digest r1-1 (accessed 2026-10-10)

Caveat: WebFetch worked, but it returns small-model summaries of the pages, not raw text. Rippling help-center docs were NOT reached (search returned no Rippling help articles). All capability claims below come from vendor marketing pages only, so none is verified against help docs. Class: V = vendor marketing, T = third-party search summary. pub_date is unknown for all sources (not shown in the retrieved content).

## Claims

1. Clock in/out works via mobile (iOS/Android), web or physical time clock device. Source: https://www.rippling.com/time-and-attendance and https://www.rippling.com/time-tracking. Publisher: Rippling. pub_date: n/a. Confidence: med. Class: V. sources_count: 2 (both vendor pages).
2. Geolocation restriction, QR code and selfie clock-in are offered. Source: WebSearch summary citing Rippling's page and Time Doctor's review (https://www.timedoctor.com/blog/?p=30760). Confidence: low-med. Class: V/T. sources_count: 2 (indirect, via search summary).
3. Job costing lets employees log time against jobs, tasks and projects, and prompts for the job at clock in/out. Source: https://www.rippling.com/time-and-attendance, https://www.rippling.com/time-tracking. Confidence: med. Class: V. sources_count: 2 (both vendor). Per-project hour reports not confirmed.
4. Overtime, meal break and other labor-law policies are applied automatically by work location, and custom pay types and job codes can be configured. Source: https://www.rippling.com/time-and-attendance; search summary. Confidence: med. Class: V. sources_count: 2.
5. Timesheets can be viewed, edited, added and approved by managers and payroll admins, including on mobile. Approved hours flow to Payroll, and timesheets are reviewable in each pay run. Source: https://www.rippling.com/time-tracking, https://www.rippling.com/time-and-attendance. Confidence: med. Class: V. sources_count: 2.
6. Managers can see who is overdue on timesheets. Source: https://www.rippling.com/time-and-attendance. Confidence: low. Class: V. sources_count: 1 (single source).
7. Automated alerts are flagged before payroll for duplicate punches, missed clock-outs, shift mismatches, overtime risk, missed breaks and policy warnings. Source: both Rippling pages above. Confidence: med. Class: V. sources_count: 2.
8. Rippling AI flags missed clock-ins/outs, overtime risks and approval backlogs, and builds custom labor-cost and overtime reports. Source: both Rippling pages above. Confidence: med. Class: V. sources_count: 2. No AI timesheet auto-fill was found on Rippling's pages. A third-party browser extension, "Rippling Autofill Time Entries" (extscope.org), exists, which suggests no native auto-fill (inference, low).
9. Integrations: native payroll and 650+ app integrations. Calendar, Jira and Slack time-entry integrations are not confirmed. Source: Rippling pages. Confidence: med. Class: V. sources_count: 2 (both vendor).
10. Audit trail is mentioned only in the FAQ, in a compliance context. Locking or pay-period close, bulk approve and multi-level approval are not mentioned on the pages. Confidence: low. Class: V. sources_count: 1.
11. Rippling Scheduling is an add-on to T&A. Source: https://www.rippling.com/time-and-attendance. Confidence: low. Class: V. sources_count: 1 (single source).
12. Pricing: Rippling publishes no T&A price. Its pricing page says most products are billed per employee per month, some include a monthly base fee, and HR/Finance/IT products such as T&A are bought separately alongside the required core Rippling Platform. Quote-only. Source: https://www.rippling.com/pricing. Confidence: high that no public price exists. Class: V. sources_count: 1.
13. Third-party price estimates (unverified by Rippling): the base platform is $8 per user per month plus a $40 monthly base; modules add about $4-12 PEPM; a 100-employee HRIS + Payroll + Benefits + T&A bundle is about $15-25 PEPM. Source: WebSearch summary of Gusto, eightx, anywherer, spotsaas and others (e.g. https://gusto.com/resources/guides/switch-payroll-providers/rippling-pricing). Publishers are competitors and aggregators, so treat as low confidence. Class: T. sources_count: 3+ but conflicting and not primary.
14. Reviews, positive: users describe time and time-off tracking as simple, with good end-user timesheets, and reviewers like customizable rules. Sources: Capterra, TrustRadius, Gartner (May 2026) and businessnewsdaily, via a search summary only. Confidence: low-med. Class: T. sources_count: 4 (as reported by the search summary, not read directly).
15. Reviews, complaints (each pattern is mostly 1 source): too many steps to submit hours (TrustRadius); page not loading or unclear whether entry saved (Capterra); manager-approval notification never arrived (Capterra); mobile app usability (TechJockey); multi-location tax tracking problems (TrustRadius); phone support costs extra and chat is delayed (businessnewsdaily); BBB "worst system" (BBB); Kimola's Google Play analysis reports login, slow and error issues (Kimola, a vendor selling reports). Customizations and reports are not intuitive and support is slow (Capterra). The only pattern with 2 independent sources is mobile or app reliability (TechJockey and Kimola). Capterra aggregate sentiment: Positive 4,511, Neutral 42, Negative 50 (all Rippling products, not T&A only). Confidence: low. Class: T. No 1-3 star filtering was possible.
16. Changes in the last 12 months: none found. Gartner May 2026 review and "2026" Capterra pages exist but contain no release notes.
