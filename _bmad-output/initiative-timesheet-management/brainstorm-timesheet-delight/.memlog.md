- (insight) Synthesis: (1) Auto-draft week + exceptions inbox for reviewers is the one idea behind 20+ ideas (prefill, ambient capture, panic mode, bot-first review). (3) Premium tier = automation + intelligence + client transparency; free tier = core sheet and reminders.
