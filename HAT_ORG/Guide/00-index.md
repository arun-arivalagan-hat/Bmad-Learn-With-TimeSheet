# BMAD Adoption Index

Entry point for the BMAD adoption docs in this repository. Problems shared by several steps are in [troubleshooting.md](troubleshooting.md). Which role runs which command, and who leads each role, is in [roles-and-commands.md](roles-and-commands.md). What the three BMAD modules are and how they differ is in [bmad-modules.md](../Modules/bmad-modules.md).

Status values: Not started, Next, In progress, Draft (written, awaiting owner review), Complete. Step numbers are not reused once a step has a doc. The owner set the user-voice research as step 9 and the product brief as step 14, so the ticket plan and build (no docs yet) are steps 15 and 16.
